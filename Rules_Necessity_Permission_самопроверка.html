Cheat sheet

Read this before you start. Two groups: rules and permission (1–4), necessity and importance (5–8).

StructureMeaningExample
have got tosomething I need to do (личная обязанность)I’ve got to leave now or I’ll be late.
be supposed tosomething people expect you to do (от вас этого ждут)We’re supposed to attend the Monday meeting.
be allowed toto have permission (разрешено)We’re allowed to work from home twice a week.
be permitted toto have permission, more formal (официальное разрешение)Visitors are permitted to enter after showing ID.
need toit is necessary for a person to do something (акцент на человеке)I need to study for my exam tonight.
it’s necessary tosomething must be done (общая необходимость)It’s necessary to wear a seat belt in a car.
it’s essential tonecessary and very important (очень важно)It’s essential to drink plenty of water.
needs to be donesomething requires action (акцент на действии, а не на человеке)The report needs to be finished by Friday.
Compare.
I’ve got to attend the meeting. / I’m supposed to attend the meeting.
Первое — личная необходимость, второе — от меня этого ждут.
I need to clean the kitchen. / The kitchen needs to be cleaned.
Одно и то же, но в первом важен человек, во втором — действие.
Watch out!
  • After these structures we use to + infinitive: supposed to jump, not supposed jumping.
  • We need it and to: It is essential to print your name. (нельзя пропускать it)
  • Negative infinitive: essential not to lose your temper. (not ставится перед to)
  • don’t need to = it isn’t necessary; aren’t allowed / permitted to = it is forbidden. (не обязательно ≠ нельзя)
  • Questions: Are we allowed to…? Do I need to…? Have I got to…?
  • Passive: needs to be finished, not to be finish. (после be — третья форма глагола)
